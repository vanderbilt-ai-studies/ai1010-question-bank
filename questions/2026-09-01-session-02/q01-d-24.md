**Question:** In an agent product such as Claude Code, what role does the decoder transformer play relative to everything else in the product?

- A. It is the whole product, and everything else is a chat window drawn around it
- B. It has been replaced by a newer architecture designed for agents
- C. It holds the working space, memory, and task lists inside its own weights
- D. It is the small piece in the middle, with a harness built around it

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. That is a fair description of a plain chat app, where the model really is nearly all there is. An agent product puts much more around the same model, while the model itself still does only what it always did: context in, one token out.
- B. Not quite. The transformer from 2017 is still the model at the center, in an agent as much as in a chat. What is new around it is the harness, not the architecture inside.
- C. Not quite. The weights are a fuzzy JPEG of the world and cannot hold your files or your task list. Those live outside the model, on disk, and reach it only when pulled into the context.
- D. Correct. The transformer still only does what it always does: context in, one token out. The harness gives it a place to work, documents to read, memory to keep, tasks to track, and a library of skills to load into its context.
