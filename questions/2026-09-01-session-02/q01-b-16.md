**Question:** Starting from the embedding for "king," you subtract the embedding for "man" and add the embedding for "woman." Where do you land?

- A. Near "queen"
- B. Back near "man"
- C. Near a genderless "sovereign" or "ruler"
- D. Near "princess"

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. Taking away the male component and adding the female one moves the location to the female royal, which is what "location is meaning" predicts. Arithmetic on positions is arithmetic on meaning.
- B. Not quite. Subtracting "man" removes that direction rather than returning to it. What is left is royalty, and adding "woman" then moves it to the female royal.
- C. Not quite. That is where you land after subtracting "man" alone. Adding "woman" takes one more step, from the genderless ruler to the female one.
- D. Not quite. A princess is royal and female but is not the counterpart to a king. The arithmetic swaps gender and keeps rank, and the same-rank female royal is a queen.
