**Question:** Why does your text have to be turned into numbers before the model can work on it?

- A. Because numbers take up less storage space than letters
- B. Because the model reads numbers faster than it reads words
- C. Because deep learning models can only ever deal with numbers
- D. Because numbers hide the content of your prompt from other users

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. Storage is not the issue. Even if text were cheaper to store, the model could not use it: the arithmetic inside a neural network needs numeric input.
- B. Not quite. Speed is not the reason either. A deep learning model cannot read words at all, quickly or slowly. Numbers are the only thing it can take in.
- C. Correct. A neural network is arithmetic on numbers, nothing else. So text has to become numbers, first as vocabulary ids and then as embeddings, before any of the machinery can touch it.
- D. Not quite. Turning text into vocabulary numbers is not encryption; anyone with the vocabulary can turn them back. The conversion exists because the model needs numbers, not because of privacy.

</details>
