**Question:** What does the token embedding stage produce for each token?

- A. The token's id number from the vocabulary
- B. A vector: the token's location in a high-dimensional space of meanings
- C. A probability that the token is the correct next word
- D. A list of the token's dictionary definitions

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. The id number is what the encoding stage produces, one step earlier. An id number is arbitrary and carries no meaning; the embedding is where meaning enters.
- B. Correct. An embedding is a list of thousands of numbers that place the token in a space where nearby tokens have similar meanings. Location is meaning, which is what lets the model work with what a word means.
- C. Not quite. Probabilities come out at the end of the pipeline, after the decoder stacks. The embedding stage is at the beginning and is about representing the tokens already in the context.
- D. Not quite. There are no dictionary entries anywhere in the model; it only handles numbers. The embedding captures meaning as a position in space, not as text.

</details>
