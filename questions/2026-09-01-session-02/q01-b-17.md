**Question:** Why does subtracting the embedding for "man" from the embedding for "king" produce something near a genderless "sovereign" or "ruler"?

- A. Because the model has a rule written into it that "king" equals "man" plus "ruler"
- B. Because "man" and "king" are stored in the same database record
- C. Because the two tokens' vocabulary id numbers are subtracted, leaving the id for "ruler"
- D. Because meaning is position, so removing the "man" direction leaves royalty

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. No such rule is written down anywhere. The relationship falls out of where the tokens were placed during training, not from a formula someone entered.
- B. Not quite. There are no records. Each token is just a point in the space, and the result comes from the geometry of those points.
- C. Not quite. Vocabulary ids are arbitrary labels, and subtracting one from another lands on nothing in particular. The arithmetic is done on the embedding vectors, where each direction through the space carries some aspect of meaning.
- D. Correct. Since location is meaning, each direction in the space corresponds to some aspect of meaning. Subtracting "man" strips out the male aspect, and what remains sits among rulers and crowns.
