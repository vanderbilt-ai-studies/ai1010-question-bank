**Question:** Suppose a model had no attention layers at all. What would happen to the meaning of "king" in "She looked across the board and said, triumphantly, 'King me'"?

- A. It would stay where the embedding put it, among monarchs, whatever the surrounding words said
- B. The MLP (multi-layer perceptron) would use the surrounding words to move it toward checkers instead
- C. The token encoding would switch to the verb form of "king" to compensate
- D. The model would be unable to produce a next token for the sentence

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. The embedding is a first guess made in isolation, and attention is the only mechanism that lets the other words move it. Without attention, "board" and "triumphantly" would have no way to reach "king".
- B. Not quite. The MLP works only on the embedding it is handed and applies what it learned in training; it does not read the other words in the context. Gathering from the context is attention's job, and nothing else does it.
- C. Not quite. Token encoding assigns the same number to "king" regardless of sense, and it happens before any meaning is worked out. It cannot respond to context.
- D. Not quite. The pipeline would still run and still produce probabilities; it would just do so with a poor sense of what each word means here. The failure would be a worse guess, not no guess.
