**Question:** When a model answers your question, there are only two places its answer can draw on. What are they?

- A. What you put in the context, and what training left in its weights
- B. What you put in the context, and whatever it can look up while answering
- C. Its weights, and a record of the conversations you have had with it before
- D. The training text kept inside it, and the prompt you just typed

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. Attention reaches everything in front of the model right now, and the MLP (multi-layer perceptron) supplies what training compressed into the weights. There is no third source, which is why the context is the lever you have.
- B. Not quite. Looking something up would need a search step, and the model as described has none: context in, one token out. Anything not in the context has to come from the weights.
- C. Not quite. Nothing from an earlier conversation reaches the model unless it is in this context. The weights do not change while you chat, and no transcript is kept inside the model.
- D. Not quite. The training text is not kept. It was compressed into weights, lossy like a JPEG image, so there is nothing to reread. And your prompt is only one part of the context.

</details>
