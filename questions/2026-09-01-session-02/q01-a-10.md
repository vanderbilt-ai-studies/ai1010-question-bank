**Question:** Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini are made by three different companies. What does the lecture's single pipeline picture claim about all three?

- A. Each one adds its own extra stage, which is why their answers differ
- B. They can share one picture because they were trained on the same text
- C. All three are decoder transformers, so one picture describes each of them
- D. Only Claude matches the picture, since the lecture drew its examples from it

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. The differences you notice come from size, training, and the product built around the model. The stages themselves, from context through to one chosen token, are the same in each.
- B. Not quite. Training text differs from company to company, and it is not what the picture is about. What the three share is the architecture: each is a decoder transformer.
- C. Correct. The decoder transformer is the architecture behind every modern chat model. Read the pipeline once and you have read all of them; what differs is scale, training, and the product wrapped around the model.
- D. Not quite. Claude turns up in the examples, but the picture was offered as the shape of a generative model in general. Every current chat model is built this way.
