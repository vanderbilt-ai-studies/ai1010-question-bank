**Question:** On the lecture's timeline, where do generative AI (artificial intelligence) and AI agents sit?

- A. In a new era that began after deep learning ended
- B. In the machine learning era, alongside random forests
- C. In the expert systems era, because they follow written rules
- D. At the leading edge of the deep learning era

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. It is tempting to give generative AI its own box, but the lecture did not. Deep learning has not ended; generative models and agents are its newest chapter, built from the same kind of neural networks.
- B. Not quite. Generative models do learn from data, which is why this sounds right. But they are deep neural networks, which puts them in the deep learning era rather than the earlier one of random forests and logistic regression.
- C. Not quite. A chat model can look as if it follows rules, but nobody wrote any rules for it. Expert systems were hand-built from human knowledge; a transformer learns everything from text.
- D. Correct. Generative AI and agents are the red arrow at the far end of deep learning: the same kind of neural network, pushed further. That is the era this course lives in.
