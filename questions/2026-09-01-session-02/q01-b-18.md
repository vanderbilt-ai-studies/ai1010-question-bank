**Question:** Mathematically, what kind of object is a token embedding?

- A. A single whole number that identifies the token
- B. A probability between 0 and 1
- C. A vector, a list of thousands of numbers
- D. A table that maps the token to its related words

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. A single number is the token encoding, the vocabulary id. The embedding replaces that id with something much richer.
- B. Not quite. Probabilities are what the model produces at the end, one per vocabulary token. The embedding is an input to the stacks, not an output.
- C. Correct. A location in a space with thousands of dimensions is specified by one number per dimension, and a list of numbers like that is a vector. That is what the decoder stacks read and refine.
- D. Not quite. Relationships between tokens are not tabulated. They are implied by distance: nearby vectors mean similar things, with no table needed.

</details>
