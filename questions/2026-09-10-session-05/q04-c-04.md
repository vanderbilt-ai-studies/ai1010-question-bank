**Question:** What is the purpose of a project manifest in organizing agent work?

- A. To copy the entire project into each model input.
- B. To guarantee that every published file is accurate.
- C. To replace the source evidence after a summary is made.
- D. To help locate relevant project material.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. A manifest helps locate relevant material. It does not imply that the contents of every listed file have been loaded.
- B. Not quite. Organization helps you find files, but accuracy still requires checking the contents against the task.
- C. Not quite. A manifest can point to sources, but it cannot substitute for their evidence. Keep important originals available to reopen.
- D. Correct. A useful manifest works with filenames and folders to guide retrieval. The agent can then read the material needed for its current step.
