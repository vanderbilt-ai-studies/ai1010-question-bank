**Question:** Why should you review a skill even when an artificial intelligence author produces polished instructions?

- A. Fluent wording is the main evidence that every required step ran
- B. Human review should judge wording before deciding the output requirements
- C. Clear prose can still omit task-specific quality requirements
- D. A procedure written by a capable model needs checking only for formatting

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. Readable instructions describe work; they do not establish execution. Inspect tool and artifact evidence for completed steps.
- B. Not quite. Your expertise should make important requirements explicit before you judge the results, rather than letting polished wording determine success.
- C. Correct. An author can organize the instructions well while missing errors or standards that your experience tells you matter.
- D. Not quite. A capable author can still miss task-specific requirements. Review the substance and inspect actual results, as well as readability.
