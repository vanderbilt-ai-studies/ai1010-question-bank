**Question:** What cost-conscious model strategy do the notes propose for developing and running a skill?

- A. Choose the cheapest model and assume instructions remove quality differences
- B. Use the most capable model for every run without examining its cost
- C. Choose a model by the length of its explanations rather than its results
- D. Develop with a capable model and test cheaper execution

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. A skill does not guarantee adequate execution on a less expensive model. Test actual performance.
- B. Not quite. A capable model can help develop the procedure, but cost and required quality still deserve comparison.
- C. Not quite. Explanation length does not establish task success or whether the operating cost is justified.
- D. Correct. A stronger authoring model may help refine a procedure that a cheaper model can execute adequately, but you must evaluate that tradeoff.
