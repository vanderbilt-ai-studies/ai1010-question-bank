**Question:** What is the role of a skill's scripts/ folder?

- A. Holding prose about when to select the skill
- B. Holding code for repeatable operations
- C. Holding the finished documents from every run
- D. Holding blank layouts for future documents

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. Selection guidance belongs in SKILL.md and its description. Scripts provide executable operations.
- B. Correct. A skill can include executable routines for operations such as extraction that recur across tasks.
- C. Not quite. Finished documents are outputs of particular tasks. Scripts contain code used to perform recurring operations.
- D. Not quite. Blank layouts are reusable assets. Scripts instead supply executable steps.

</details>
