**Question:** Why should a lecture-notes skill keep its reusable procedure separate from one lecture's transcript?

- A. The procedure should reproduce the same notes each time
- B. The transcript must contain the skill's selection metadata
- C. The procedure must apply to different lectures
- D. The transcript becomes a reusable template after one run

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. Reusability means applying a method to changing inputs, not forcing every lecture to produce identical content.
- B. Not quite. Selection metadata describes the skill. A transcript is source material and should not have to define the skill's identity.
- C. Correct. The skill encodes how to do recurring work; the transcript supplies the content that changes from run to run.
- D. Not quite. A source transcript still describes a particular lecture. Repeated use does not turn its content into a general output template.

</details>
