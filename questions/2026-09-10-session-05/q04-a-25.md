**Question:** A commercial model gives a better answer after spending longer on a problem. You know research models can use hidden recurrence. What can you conclude about this commercial model?

- A. It uses the same recurrent block as the published research model.
- B. It added new independent parameters while working on this request.
- C. Its printed explanation fully specifies the hidden numerical process.
- D. Its architecture remains uncertain from that behavior alone.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Similar behavior does not establish identical internals. A published research architecture is evidence about that research system.
- B. Not quite. More inference time does not establish a larger parameter set. Existing weights can support additional computation.
- C. Not quite. A written explanation is not a complete record of internal vector operations. It does not disclose the architecture merely by describing an answer.
- D. Correct. Longer processing and a better answer are observations of behavior. They do not identify the undisclosed mechanism that produced the result.
