**Question:** Why can a skill written in a portable instruction format still fail after being copied to another host?

- A. The copied instructions retrain the destination model with different weights
- B. The destination may lack required programs or permissions
- C. The copied folder moves the original computer's permissions to the new host
- D. The destination uses the original run's output in place of the new task input

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. A skill supplies task guidance; loading its instructions does not retrain the model. Check the destination environment and performance.
- B. Correct. Portable instructions do not ensure portable executable dependencies or access. Inspect the new environment and run a representative task.
- C. Not quite. Permissions belong to the operating environment. Copying instructions does not transfer or grant the access required in another host.
- D. Not quite. The reusable procedure, each run's input, and its output have different roles. Transferring a skill does not make an earlier output the new input.
