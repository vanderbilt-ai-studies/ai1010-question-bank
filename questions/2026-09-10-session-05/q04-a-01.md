**Question:** What does tokenization do to text before a decoder transformer processes it?

- A. Assigns a probability to each complete answer.
- B. Splits text into pieces with vocabulary identifiers.
- C. Converts each sentence into a verified factual claim.
- D. Stores each paragraph as an independent learned weight.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. Next-token probabilities come later in the pipeline. Tokenization prepares the text for numerical processing.
- B. Correct. A token can be a word or a piece of a word. Its identifier lets the model map that piece to a numerical representation.
- C. Not quite. Tokenization handles the representation of text. It does not check whether a sentence is true.
- D. Not quite. Learned weights are model parameters. Input paragraphs become token sequences rather than new weights.

</details>
