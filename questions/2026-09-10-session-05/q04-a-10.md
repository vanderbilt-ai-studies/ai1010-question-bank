**Question:** In recurrent-depth processing, what is repeatedly transformed before an output token is selected?

- A. An internal numerical representation.
- B. A newly printed sentence in the response.
- C. An updated set of training labels.
- D. A freshly expanded token vocabulary.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. The recurrent block refines hidden vectors. Those passes can occur without adding words to a visible reasoning trace.
- B. Not quite. Printing additional text grows the output sequence. Hidden recurrence works on representations before output selection.
- C. Not quite. Training labels concern learning from examples. Recurrent-depth inference applies an already trained block to a representation.
- D. Not quite. Reusing a computation block does not require expanding the vocabulary. It increases processing of the internal state.

</details>
