**Question:** Why can a small skill consist of SKILL.md alone?

- A. Supporting folders would prevent the catalog from selecting it
- B. Its procedure may need no additional supporting files
- C. The central file is intended to hold metadata without a procedure
- D. A short procedure gains executable behavior from its filename

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. Supporting files are compatible with progressive disclosure. They are opened when needed rather than preventing selection.
- B. Correct. A focused procedure can fit in the central file. References, assets, and scripts are added when they serve a useful purpose.
- C. Not quite. SKILL.md contains both identifying metadata and instructions. A one-file skill still needs its reusable procedure.
- D. Not quite. Naming an instruction document does not turn it into a script. Some useful skills rely on instructions without bundled executable code.

</details>
