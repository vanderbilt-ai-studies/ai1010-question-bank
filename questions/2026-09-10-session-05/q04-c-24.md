**Question:** A support report describes a familiar broken link, but you cannot establish which of two course sections the sender means. What should the workflow do?

- A. Repair both sections so that one of the changes will reach the intended target.
- B. Repair the first section listed because the symptom matches a known case.
- C. Choose the section with the most familiar title and report a verified repair.
- D. Resolve the section or escalate the ambiguity before repairing.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Changing both expands the intervention without resolving the target. The workflow first needs to establish the intended section.
- B. Not quite. A known symptom does not identify the correct course area. Acting on list order risks changing the wrong target.
- C. Not quite. A plausible title is not sufficient target evidence. Reporting verification would also be premature before checking the affected result.
- D. Correct. The repair boundary includes a known target as well as a known procedure. Preserve the collected evidence and escalate if the section remains uncertain.
