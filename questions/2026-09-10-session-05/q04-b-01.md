**Question:** Which file holds a skill's central instructions?

- A. SKILL.md
- B. references/writing-guide.md
- C. assets/notes-template.md
- D. outputs/lecture-notes.md

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. This is the central file that combines identifying metadata and the reusable procedure.
- B. Not quite. A referenced guide can provide detailed supporting instructions. It supports the central procedure rather than replacing SKILL.md.
- C. Not quite. A template provides a reusable starting structure. The central instructions explain how to use that structure.
- D. Not quite. Generated notes are the result of one run. Their filename does not make them the reusable procedure for future runs.

</details>
