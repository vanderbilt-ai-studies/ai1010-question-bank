**Question:** Your notes skill produces readable summaries but repeatedly omits challenging study questions. You know those questions are essential. What is the best next step?

- A. Specify the missing requirement and evaluate revised outputs
- B. Assume the polished summaries show that the original skill meets the need
- C. Switch to unattended operation so repeated runs eventually include the questions
- D. Replace the test transcripts with easier ones before comparing the summaries

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. Your expertise identifies the needed quality criterion. Make it explicit, then test whether the revision changes the artifacts as intended.
- B. Not quite. Readable prose is only one quality dimension. The repeated omission shows an important requirement is unmet.
- C. Not quite. Repeated operation does not reliably repair an omitted requirement and can keep producing the same inadequate output.
- D. Not quite. Easier inputs can conceal the problem. Evaluate whether the revised procedure meets the actual requirement on useful examples.
