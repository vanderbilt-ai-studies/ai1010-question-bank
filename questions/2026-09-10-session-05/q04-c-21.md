**Question:** You ask an agent to fix a reading link. The new link opens a document with the expected title, but two documents share that title. What should you check next?

- A. Read the destination and compare it with the intended reading.
- B. Accept the repair because a successful click establishes the right content.
- C. Check the operation log for a completed update and accept that as verification.
- D. Compare the displayed title again and omit reading either document itself.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. The title is ambiguous, so content readback is the evidence that can identify the intended document. A successful click alone does not settle the issue.
- B. Not quite. A successful click shows that something opened. It does not distinguish between two documents with the same title.
- C. Not quite. A log showing an update establishes that an operation was attempted or executed. It does not establish that the saved link reaches the intended content.
- D. Not quite. Repeating an ambiguous title check cannot resolve which document is linked. The content must be inspected.
