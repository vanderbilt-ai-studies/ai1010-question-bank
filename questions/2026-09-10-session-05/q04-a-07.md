**Question:** Which aspect of generation does the temperature setting directly adjust?

- A. The number of independent weights in the trained network.
- B. The maximum amount of text that fits into the context.
- C. The amount of computation allowed before an answer begins.
- D. The concentration of the distribution used for token sampling.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Changing temperature does not rebuild the network. Parameter count concerns the model architecture and its learned values.
- B. Not quite. Context capacity and temperature are separate settings. Temperature changes how token choices are distributed.
- C. Not quite. That describes a thinking budget. Temperature can affect sampled paths, but it is not itself a compute budget.
- D. Correct. Lower temperature favors higher-scoring choices more strongly. Higher temperature spreads probability more broadly.
