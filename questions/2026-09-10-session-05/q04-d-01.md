**Question:** When you improve a skill with artificial intelligence assistance, what does human expertise contribute?

- A. A guarantee that the first draft will work reliably
- B. Proof that the agent executed every requested step
- C. Requirements about important errors and quality
- D. Automatic installation in every compatible environment

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. Expert guidance improves the requirements, but the procedure still needs testing on actual tasks.
- B. Not quite. A person can specify the steps, but you need evidence of execution to establish that they happened.
- C. Correct. Your experience identifies misunderstandings, costly mistakes, and standards that the agent may fail to infer.
- D. Not quite. Human expertise shapes the procedure; installation and environment compatibility require separate checks.

</details>
