**Question:** What information initially helps an agent choose a relevant skill from a catalog?

- A. The catalog names and short descriptions
- B. The full procedure of every installed skill
- C. A ranking by when the skills were installed
- D. The largest collection of bundled examples

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. These give the agent an initial account of each skill's purpose and applicability before loading detailed instructions.
- B. Not quite. The detailed procedure is loaded after a skill is selected. Requiring every complete procedure would defeat staged loading.
- C. Not quite. Installation order does not establish whether a procedure fits the requested work. The description supplies relevance information.
- D. Not quite. Many examples may help a procedure, but their number does not identify whether its purpose matches the task.

</details>
