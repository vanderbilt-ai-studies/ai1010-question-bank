**Question:** Why does a fresh subagent need both a focused assignment and relevant background?

- A. Fresh context automatically includes every decision from the main conversation.
- B. A narrow task still depends on facts it may not have received.
- C. A bounded assignment guarantees that the returned finding needs no checking.
- D. Loading unrelated history is a substitute for supplying the relevant evidence.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. Fresh context should not be assumed to contain unsupplied facts. Relevant background has to be provided or made accessible.
- B. Correct. Focus limits the question, but it does not supply missing evidence. Give the subagent enough context and check its returned finding.
- C. Not quite. Narrow scope can help manage context, but it does not guarantee accuracy. The resulting answer still needs verification.
- D. Not quite. A larger input does not necessarily contain the evidence the question requires. Select the background that supports the bounded assignment.
