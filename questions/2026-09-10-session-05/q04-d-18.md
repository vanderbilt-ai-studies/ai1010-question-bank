**Question:** Why should you reserve some development time and usage budget after a skill has been drafted?

- A. A longer drafting session guarantees that later errors cannot occur
- B. Checking outputs can be replaced by choosing the most capable authoring model
- C. Checking outputs can reveal changes the procedure still needs
- D. Installing the finished folder makes all further verification unnecessary

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. More drafting alone does not establish reliability. Evaluation can reveal problems the draft did not anticipate.
- B. Not quite. A capable model can help develop the procedure, but its involvement does not establish that outputs satisfy your requirements.
- C. Correct. Useful development includes testing and revision, so spending the entire budget on the first draft leaves important work undone.
- D. Not quite. Installation makes a skill available but does not prove that its outputs meet the task requirements.

</details>
