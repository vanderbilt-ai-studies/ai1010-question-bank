**Question:** Why should important original evidence remain available after conversation compaction?

- A. The summary guarantees that every source will be reopened automatically.
- B. The summary turns earlier tentative claims into verified project facts.
- C. The summary can omit details that later become relevant.
- D. The summary requires all originals to be pasted into every later request.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. A summary does not guarantee later retrieval. Keeping source locations and originals available makes reopening possible when a detail is needed.
- B. Not quite. Summarizing a claim does not validate it. Original evidence may be needed to check whether a tentative statement was established.
- C. Correct. Compaction selects what to preserve in a shorter account. Original evidence lets you recover a detail that the summary did not retain.
- D. Not quite. Sources can remain accessible without being included in every input. Reopen the evidence needed for the next step.

</details>
