**Question:** A known, authorized repair has been applied to the intended course section. The agent has not read back the object and has two possible recipient addresses. What should happen before a success message is sent?

- A. Verify the repaired behavior and establish the intended recipient.
- B. Use the first address and describe the attempted change as a verified result.
- C. Send the same success message to both addresses to cover the uncertainty.
- D. Ask the agent to confirm its confidence and reuse the most plausible address.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. An applied operation is not yet a verified outcome, and recipient ambiguity remains unresolved. Check both before communicating success and identify the automated responder.
- B. Not quite. List order does not establish the intended recipient. The claimed result also needs evidence from the affected object.
- C. Not quite. Broad delivery does not resolve who should receive the result. The workflow restricts the recipient and validates the repair before responding.
- D. Not quite. Confidence cannot substitute for inspecting the repaired behavior. A plausible address also needs to be established as the intended destination.
