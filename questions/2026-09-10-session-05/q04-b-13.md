**Question:** Why is the number of installed skills insufficient to determine whether a skill catalog is useful?

- A. Selection also depends on description quality, overlap, and context use.
- B. Selection depends on the total length of all previous generated outputs.
- C. Selection improves whenever more skills cover the same recurring task.
- D. Selection quality follows from storing the catalog in one project folder.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. The same count can describe clearly separated procedures or a confusing set of overlapping ones. The notes give no universal maximum for useful installed skills.
- B. Not quite. Past outputs describe particular runs, while selection begins with skill descriptions. Output volume does not establish whether the catalog offers clear, relevant guidance.
- C. Not quite. Overlapping coverage can make the intended procedure harder to identify. More entries do not by themselves establish a more useful catalog.
- D. Not quite. Project scope can fit local conventions, but storage location does not establish description quality or distinguish overlapping procedures. Assess how the skills fit the work.
