**Question:** Why is the length of a displayed reasoning explanation an incomplete measure of internal computation?

- A. Internal vector processing need not appear as additional displayed tokens.
- B. The displayed steps report new weights learned during the current reply.
- C. The explanation describes context capacity rather than the work performed.
- D. The displayed words record the original embeddings before model processing.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. A system can perform numerical work without printing a corresponding sequence of words. The displayed explanation is therefore an incomplete measurement.
- B. Not quite. The notes distinguish inference computation from learning parameters. A written explanation does not report a sequence of training updates during the reply.
- C. Not quite. A written explanation is generated output, not a measure of the context limit. Its incompleteness comes from hidden processing that may not be displayed.
- D. Not quite. Embeddings are numerical representations transformed inside the model. The explanation is generated after model processing, not a printout of those initial vectors.
