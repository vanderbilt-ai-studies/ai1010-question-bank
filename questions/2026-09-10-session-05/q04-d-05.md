**Question:** What comparison is central to evaluating whether a skill improves task performance?

- A. Matched tasks with and without the skill
- B. Different tasks using unrelated models and instructions
- C. The lengths of the skill file and the generated answer
- D. The confidence of the author before and after editing

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. Keeping inputs and relevant settings comparable helps you interpret differences in the resulting artifacts.
- B. Not quite. Several factors change at once, so the resulting difference gives weak evidence about the skill itself.
- C. Not quite. Length does not measure whether the output meets the task requirements.
- D. Not quite. Confidence can change without performance improving. Evaluate actual outputs against defined criteria.

</details>
