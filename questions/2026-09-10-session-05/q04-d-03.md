**Question:** What evidence supports a claim that the requested Skill Creator procedure was actually used?

- A. A final message that prominently names Skill Creator
- B. A skill folder with a polished title and complete instructions
- C. A record showing that the agent read the short catalog description
- D. Records of loaded instructions and completed required steps

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Naming a procedure in generated text does not establish that its instructions were loaded or its steps performed.
- B. Not quite. A finished document can exist without the requested authoring procedure having been followed.
- C. Not quite. The description helps select a skill. Evidence of selection alone does not establish that the detailed instructions were loaded and their required steps completed.
- D. Correct. Check evidence that the procedure was loaded and that its required work actually occurred, including relevant evaluation artifacts.

</details>
