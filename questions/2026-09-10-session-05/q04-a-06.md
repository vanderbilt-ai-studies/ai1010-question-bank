**Question:** Immediately before next-token selection, what does the decoder pipeline provide?

- A. A set of independently verified sentences to copy.
- B. A replacement vocabulary trained on the latest prompt.
- C. A distribution assigning probabilities to vocabulary tokens.
- D. A complete response with every later token already fixed.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. The decoder calculates token scores or probabilities. Their calculation is different from verifying sentences against evidence.
- B. Not quite. Ordinary generation uses the trained vocabulary and weights. A prompt does not train a replacement vocabulary.
- C. Correct. The output stage gives the probabilities used to choose the next token. Selection then extends the generated sequence.
- D. Not quite. The next token is selected from the current distribution. Later distributions depend on the sequence as it develops.

</details>
