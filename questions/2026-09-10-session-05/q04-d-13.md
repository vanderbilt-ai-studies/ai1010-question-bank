**Question:** Why should you keep inputs and relevant settings similar in runs with and without a skill?

- A. To ensure that both runs produce exactly the same wording
- B. To replace a comparison of quality with a comparison of answer length
- C. To let successful installation serve as evidence that the skill improves quality
- D. To make differences more informative about the skill

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. The purpose is to compare performance, not force identical text. Different wording can meet the same quality criteria.
- B. Not quite. Similar conditions help you interpret performance, but output length does not replace defined task-specific quality criteria.
- C. Not quite. Installation makes the skill available. Comparable runs still require inspecting actual outputs to evaluate improvement.
- D. Correct. When the task and conditions are comparable, observed changes provide better evidence about the added guidance.
