**Question:** Which information belongs in a compact working record for an unfinished project?

- A. The preferred output style and unrelated personal examples.
- B. Goals, decisions, progress, issues, sources, and next steps.
- C. The complete contents of every file that the agent can access.
- D. The intended final answer with every unfinished check omitted.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. A recurring style preference can be useful, but it does not tell you what happened in this project or what remains to do.
- B. Correct. These details allow work to resume with its purpose and current state intact. Source locations also support reopening evidence when the summary is insufficient.
- C. Not quite. A working record preserves useful state rather than duplicating an entire project. Relevant originals can be retrieved when needed.
- D. Not quite. A desired outcome is not a record of progress. Unresolved checks matter because they distinguish unfinished work from verified completion.
