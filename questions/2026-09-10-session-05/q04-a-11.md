**Question:** Why is raising temperature different from giving a model a larger thinking budget?

- A. Temperature changes context capacity; a budget changes weights.
- B. Temperature adds layers; a budget enlarges token embeddings.
- C. Temperature checks facts; a budget chooses more varied words.
- D. Temperature reshapes sampling; a budget allocates computation.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Temperature does not set context capacity, and an inference budget does not retrain weights. They concern sampling and available computation.
- B. Not quite. These are architectural changes. Neither follows merely from adjusting a sampling setting or inference budget.
- C. Not quite. Temperature is not a truth-checking mechanism. A thinking budget allocates computation rather than defining sampling variety.
- D. Correct. The settings affect different mechanisms. Sampling can change a reasoning path and its length, but it does not directly specify a time or token budget.
