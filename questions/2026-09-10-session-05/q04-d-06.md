**Question:** When should you define the required qualities of an output in a skill evaluation?

- A. After choosing the output that looks most impressive
- B. After the skill has been running unattended for several weeks
- C. Before judging the outputs from the evaluation
- D. After a user reports a failure in the deployed procedure

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. Choosing standards after seeing a preferred result can reward appearance instead of the intended task requirements.
- B. Not quite. You need success criteria during development, before relying on repeated unattended operation.
- C. Correct. Advance criteria give you a stable basis for inspecting results and deciding whether the skill helps.
- D. Not quite. Reported failures can improve later tests, but initial evaluation also needs explicit success criteria.

</details>
