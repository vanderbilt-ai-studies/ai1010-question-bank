**Question:** What distinguishes hidden recurrence from the autoregressive output loop?

- A. Hidden recurrence trains weights; autoregression trains embeddings.
- B. Hidden recurrence refines vectors; autoregression extends the sequence.
- C. Hidden recurrence selects full answers; autoregression selects paragraphs.
- D. Hidden recurrence enlarges context limits; autoregression enlarges layers.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. Both can describe inference with already trained weights. Their distinction concerns internal processing versus growth of the output sequence.
- B. Correct. A recurrent pass can refine numerical representations before selection. Autoregression adds a selected token that shapes subsequent generation.
- C. Not quite. Neither mechanism requires selecting a complete answer or paragraph at once. The generation process studied here advances by tokens.
- D. Not quite. Those are capacity and architecture changes. The two loops instead describe what is repeated during processing.

</details>
