**Question:** What must you establish after saving a skill folder before relying on it in the intended host?

- A. That the folder has been copied into several unrelated projects
- B. That the host has discovered and enabled the skill
- C. That the agent reports successful installation in its final message
- D. That its text is identical to the version on a different machine

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. Extra copies do not establish that the intended host has discovered and enabled the skill.
- B. Correct. Saving files and making them available for use in a host are distinct events. Check the destination environment.
- C. Not quite. A completion claim is generated text. Inspect whether the destination actually discovered and enabled the skill.
- D. Not quite. Matching text can help preserve a procedure, but does not establish that the destination can use it.

</details>
