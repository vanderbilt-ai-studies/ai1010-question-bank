**Question:** What should the final response in a bounded support workflow communicate?

- A. An attempted repair to every available course contact.
- B. The verified result and the responder's automated identity.
- C. A planned repair described as completed by a human operator.
- D. The original report with the verification outcome left unstated.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. An attempt is not a verified result, and broad delivery can misdirect the message. Check success and use the intended recipient.
- B. Correct. The response should go to the intended recipient after validation. Identifying the automated responder makes the nature of that communication clear.
- C. Not quite. A plan does not establish completion, and describing an automated responder as human obscures who acted. Report the verified result accurately.
- D. Not quite. Repeating the report does not tell the recipient what happened. The workflow calls for communicating the result that has actually been checked.
