**Question:** What is a suitable task for a fresh subagent in a long project?

- A. Infer a missing acceptance decision from the project title.
- B. Expand a document check into completing the remaining project.
- C. Investigate a bounded question with relevant context.
- D. Certify a proposed repair from its description before it runs.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. A title helps identify a project but does not supply its decisions. Give the subagent relevant decision evidence for its bounded question.
- B. Not quite. A fresh subagent is useful for a bounded question. Expanding the assignment adds scope beyond the focused check it was meant to perform.
- C. Correct. A focused task lets a subagent work with the information it needs and return a concise finding. Fresh context does not remove the need to check the answer.
- D. Not quite. A proposed repair describes an intended action, not an observed outcome. A subagent still needs evidence and its finding still needs checking.

</details>
