**Question:** What conclusion is supported by three successful tests of a newly edited skill?

- A. The procedure has enough evidence to begin unattended operation without failure tests
- B. The procedure has enough evidence to skip a representative test after transfer
- C. The procedure improved performance even though no run without it was compared
- D. The revision worked on those examples and merits further testing

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Three successful examples leave failure behavior untested. Persistent operation needs explicit operating conditions and failure testing.
- B. Not quite. A new host can differ in tools, dependencies, and permissions. Success in the original environment does not establish performance there.
- C. Not quite. Successful outputs show that the tested runs met requirements. A matched comparison with and without the skill is needed to assess its added value.
- D. Correct. A small set of successes can guide iteration and build limited confidence without proving broad reliability.
