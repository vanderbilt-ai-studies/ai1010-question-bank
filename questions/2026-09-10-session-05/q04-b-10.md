**Question:** Which scope best suits a skill that follows conventions specific to one project?

- A. Personal scope with the project conventions applied everywhere
- B. Project-local scope where those conventions apply
- C. Personal scope with project-specific selection guidance removed
- D. Project-local scope with its reusable procedure removed

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. Personal reuse is suitable when assumptions fit the receiving projects. Applying unusual conventions everywhere ignores that condition.
- B. Correct. A skill tied to a project's assumptions fits project-local scope. It remains reusable across tasks in that project.
- C. Not quite. Removing the guidance makes its assumptions harder to recognize. Broader availability does not make those assumptions broadly appropriate.
- D. Not quite. Local placement is useful, but removing the procedure discards the reusable expertise. Location and instructions serve different purposes.

</details>
