**Question:** Per the lecture, what lever for making a model larger was already established in Session 3, before today's addition?

- A. Adding more decoder stacks to the pipeline.
- B. Feeding a stack's output back into itself for extra passes.
- C. Increasing the temperature setting used at output time.
- D. Wider MLP (multi-layer perceptron) matrices and a larger embedding space.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. This is today's addition (depth), not the Session 3 lever. Session 3's lever was about width, not the number of stacks.
- B. Not quite. This describes looping, a separate architectural idea introduced today, not the Session 3 lever about model size.
- C. Not quite. Temperature affects sampling randomness at the final output step and is unrelated to what makes a model tier larger.
- D. Correct. This is the lever Session 3 established: a larger model tier typically means wider MLP matrices and a bigger embedding space, both about the width of the pipeline rather than its length.
