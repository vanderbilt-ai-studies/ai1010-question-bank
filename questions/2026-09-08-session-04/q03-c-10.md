**Question:** In the 'no pencils or pens' analogy, what is a student in that scenario forced to rely on if they cannot write anything down or use a computer?

- A. A pocket calculator kept for exactly this situation, to store numbers and recall them
- B. Their own memory alone -- 'in their mouth' in the analogy -- until it becomes unsustainable
- C. A neighboring student who agrees to hold the information for them instead
- D. A phone camera, used to photograph the board and review the images later

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. The analogy rules out computers as well as pencils and pens, so a calculator is out too. The point is that nothing external is available at all.
- B. Correct. With no external notes allowed, everything has to be carried in memory, and the lecture's point is how quickly that becomes unsustainable -- exactly the position a chat session is in.
- C. Not quite. The analogy does not introduce a helper. It is about one person with no external way to record anything, which maps onto a single chat session with no outside notes.
- D. Not quite. A phone is a computer, and the analogy rules those out along with pencils and pens. Nothing external is allowed in the scenario.
