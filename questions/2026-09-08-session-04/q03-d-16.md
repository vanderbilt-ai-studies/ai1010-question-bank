**Question:** In the live skill-editing discussion, which single word in the lecture-notes skill's instructions was identified as the source of the problem?

- A. "Tutorial"
- B. "Background"
- C. "Detailed"
- D. "Narrative"

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. "Tutorial" appeared in the same phrase, but the actual problem was pinned on a different word in that instruction.
- B. Not quite. "Background" was not the word singled out -- it appeared in the proposed replacement instruction, not in the diagnosis of what was wrong.
- C. Not quite. "Detailed" describes the symptom students complained about (not enough detail), but it is not the word blamed inside the skill's own instructions.
- D. Correct. The problem was traced to the word "narrative" in the instruction "tutorial-style narrative prose," with the argument that the skill should ask for expository writing instead.
