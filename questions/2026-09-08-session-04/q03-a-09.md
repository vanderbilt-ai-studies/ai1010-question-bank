**Question:** Who actually chose seat-minder's technology stack (JavaScript, Python, Vercel, Neon)?

- A. The agent, working from the requirements it was given
- B. A committee of teaching assistants who reviewed several options
- C. The instructor, from years of experience with each technology
- D. Vanderbilt's IT department, following a mandated technology list

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. The notes credit the agent with the choice: requirements such as running on Vercel and needing a database of a particular kind were supplied, and the agent selected the technologies to satisfy them.
- B. Not quite. No committee or teaching-assistant review of the stack appears in the notes; the choice is attributed to the agent.
- C. Not quite. The notes contrast exactly this: requirements were given rather than every technology being personally known, and that is part of why the build moved so fast.
- D. Not quite. No institutional mandate is mentioned. The technologies follow from the requirements the agent was working to.
