**Question:** Why does the lecture treat GPT-style and Anthropic-style image handling as two genuinely different approaches, rather than saying one is simply better across the board?

- A. Because one approach gives a model image understanding and the other does not
- B. Because the two approaches train the same way but run on different hardware
- C. Because the difference shows up in training but not in what a model can do
- D. Because joint training and a later projection step lead to different capabilities

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Both approaches end up with a model that understands images. What the lecture contrasts is how each one got there.
- B. Not quite. The difference is in the training process itself -- images learned jointly from the start versus added through a later projection step -- not in the hardware.
- C. Not quite. This is the tempting reading, but the lecture ties the training difference to real downstream consequences rather than treating it as an implementation detail.
- D. Correct. How a model learned to see shapes what it is good at afterward: the lecture links the difference to strength at generating images and at operating software visually.
