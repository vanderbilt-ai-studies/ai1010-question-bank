**Question:** What implementation guidance was added after seat-minder's North Star was stated?

- A. Model it on an airline seat-reservation system, where a seat is kept or changed
- B. Build it as a native mobile app distributed through the iOS and Android stores
- C. Use only open-source models and open-source libraries for every part of the build
- D. Require every student to create an individual account with a username and password

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. That is the analogy the notes give, alongside tracking attendance so repeated absences can be followed up and making the app robust enough to survive real use.
- B. Not quite. The notes describe a Vercel-hosted web backend that students reach from a QR code, with no native mobile app anywhere in the picture.
- C. Not quite. No open-source-only constraint appears in the notes. The guidance covered the seating analogy, attendance tracking, and robustness.
- D. Not quite. The described check-in is a QR scan and a seat tap, with no account creation or password step in between.
