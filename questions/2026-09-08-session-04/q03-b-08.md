**Question:** By the original working definition described in the lecture, roughly when did the instructor argue the field actually crossed the AGI (artificial general intelligence) threshold?

- A. At the moment Astra was released, during this class session.
- B. It has not been crossed yet by any released model.
- C. Sometime in the era of expert systems, decades ago.
- D. Roughly 18 months before this lecture, largely unnoticed.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. The argument placed the crossing well before this release. Astra came up as the occasion for revisiting the definition, not as the moment the bar was met.
- B. Not quite. This is the opposite of the argument made in class, which was that the line had already been passed under the older definition and simply went unremarked.
- C. Not quite. The notes place the crossing far more recently -- about a year and a half before this lecture -- not in an earlier era of AI.
- D. Correct. That is the claim made in class, under the older and more modest definition of AGI. It is offered as the instructor's own reading of the timeline rather than a settled finding in the field.
