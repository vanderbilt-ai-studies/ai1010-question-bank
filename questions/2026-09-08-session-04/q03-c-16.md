**Question:** In the demo's second attempt, pasting the rubric helped, but grading a second student's submission in the same chat conversation introduced a new problem. What was it?

- A. The rubric had to be pasted in again before every new submission
- B. The chat session ran out of room to hold the rubric and submissions
- C. The model forgot the rubric once it had finished the first submission
- D. The first grading stayed in context, so the second was judged partly against it

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. That is not the problem identified -- the rubric stayed in context. The issue was what else stayed in context alongside it, namely the first grading.
- B. Not quite. Running out of space is not the failure described. The problem was the content that carried forward, not capacity.
- C. Not quite. The rubric was still available. What also stayed available was the first submission's grade and comments, and that is what shifted the second result.
- D. Correct. Everything earlier in a chat can influence what comes next, so a submission graded right after another is no longer judged purely on its own merits -- it can come out harsher or more lenient because of what preceded it.
