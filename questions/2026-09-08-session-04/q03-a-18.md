**Question:** How does seat-minder indicate which students are comfortable being cold-called?

- A. By listing their names in a footer at the bottom of the screen
- B. By hiding students who opted out from the seating chart entirely
- C. With a distinct highlight, described as 'OK to call on,' for students who opt in
- D. By ranking students from most to least willing on a leaderboard

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. No footer list appears in the notes. The opt-in shows up on the seating chart itself, where the rest of the information already lives.
- B. Not quite. Nobody is hidden. The chart still shows every seat; students who opt in simply get a distinct highlight on top of it.
- C. Correct. Students mark separately whether they are comfortable being cold-called, and that highlight is what makes it possible to choose someone confidently rather than at random.
- D. Not quite. No ranking appears in the notes. What is described is a yes-or-no opt-in shown as a highlight, not a scored ordering.
