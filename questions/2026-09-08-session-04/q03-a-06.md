**Question:** A student has now missed more than one class session in a row. Based on how seat-minder's attendance tracking is described in the notes, what should happen next?

- A. The student is automatically dropped from the course roster
- B. Nothing in particular, since seat-minder only records who is present each day
- C. The student's seat is reassigned to someone else for the rest of the semester
- D. The instructor follows up with that student personally

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. No automatic drop appears in the notes. The consequence described is a personal follow-up, not an administrative action.
- B. Not quite. Attendance is recorded through seat-minder precisely so a pattern like this surfaces, rather than being logged and then left alone.
- C. Not quite. The airline-style model lets you keep your seat or pick a new one each class, so no seat is taken away over absences.
- D. Correct. The notes say a no-show over more than one class period triggers a personal follow-up, which is part of why attendance is being recorded this way this semester.
