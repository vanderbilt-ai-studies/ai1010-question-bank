**Question:** Why is it useful that tapping a raised hand also shows the photos and names of neighboring seats, not just the student whose hand is raised?

- A. It fills otherwise empty space on the instructor's screen
- B. It lets the instructor take attendance for the whole row at once
- C. It shows which nearby students have opted in to being cold-called
- D. It supports calling on that student or on someone sitting nearby

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. The notes present this as a working part of cold-calling, not as a way to use up room on the display.
- B. Not quite. Attendance comes from check-in, which has already happened by the time anyone raises a hand, so nothing about tapping a hand records it.
- C. Not quite. The 'OK to call on' highlight is already visible on the chart for everyone who opted in, so tapping a raised hand is not what reveals it.
- D. Correct. Seeing the neighbors alongside the raised hand leaves you a choice: take the hand that is up, or bring in someone close by who has not spoken yet.
