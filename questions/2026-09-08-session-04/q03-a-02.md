**Question:** How do students check in with seat-minder at the start of class?

- A. They scan a QR code and tap their seat on a chart of the room
- B. They type their VUnetID into a web form
- C. They raise their hand and the instructor marks them present
- D. They swipe a student ID card at the door

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. That is the flow the notes describe: scan the QR code, tap the seat you are sitting in on the seating chart, and you are checked in.
- B. Not quite. Typing an identifier is not part of the flow described in the notes; check-in runs through a QR scan and a seat tap instead.
- C. Not quite. Raising a hand belongs to the later cold-calling view, not to how the initial check-in happens.
- D. Not quite. No card swipe appears in the notes; the described entry point is the QR code and the seating chart.
