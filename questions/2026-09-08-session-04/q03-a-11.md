**Question:** What specific engineering problem did seat-minder face with around 120 students potentially checking in within the same few seconds?

- A. The QR code image failing to render correctly on some students' phones
- B. Students being unable to find the check-in link on their own
- C. A concurrency problem: two check-ins racing for the same seat or row
- D. The seating chart forgetting a student's seat between one class and the next

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. No rendering failure appears in the notes. The problem described happens after students are already scanning and tapping.
- B. Not quite. Findability is not raised as an issue; the difficulty starts once many students are checking in at once.
- C. Correct. Many check-ins landing in the same few seconds can collide, with two students claiming one seat or two requests updating the same database row at the same instant.
- D. Not quite. Keeping a seat or picking a new one is expected behavior in the airline-style model, not the failure the notes are describing.
