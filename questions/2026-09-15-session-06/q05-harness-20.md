**Question:** What makes verification different from permission checking?

- A. Verification authorizes actions; permissions judge completed results
- B. Verification checks fluency; permissions check factual accuracy
- C. Verification checks model size; permissions check the task instructions
- D. Verification assesses results; permissions govern allowed actions

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. This reverses their roles. Permissions constrain what may execute, while verification checks whether results satisfy the task.
- B. Not quite. Fluent text can still be wrong or incomplete. Permissions govern executable actions rather than factual accuracy.
- C. Not quite. Model size is not the definition of result verification. Access controls govern allowed operations, and results must be checked against the task.
- D. Correct. An action can be permitted and still produce an incorrect or incomplete result. These checks therefore serve different purposes.

</details>
