**Question:** Which item supplies reusable guidance rather than external access?

- A. An available file-reading tool
- B. A skill with task instructions
- C. A granted file-reading permission
- D. A connector to an outside service

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. A tool provides an action that can be invoked. Reusable procedural guidance has a different role.
- B. Correct. A skill describes how to approach the work. Tools and permissions are still needed to execute its action steps.
- C. Not quite. Permission authorizes access to a resource. It does not itself provide the procedure for doing the task.
- D. Not quite. A connector exposes an integration. Guidance for using it can be supplied by a skill.

</details>
