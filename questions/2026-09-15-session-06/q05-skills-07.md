**Question:** After saving a specific change to a skill, what is the next step in the demonstrated workflow?

- A. Package the previous output as the final result
- B. Treat the saved instructions as proof of success
- C. Run a test and examine the resulting output
- D. Change the model before trying the procedure

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. A previous output does not show what the changed skill does. Test the revised version before treating the work as complete.
- B. Not quite. Saving confirms that you changed the procedure. The next check is whether using it produces the intended result.
- C. Correct. Testing connects the written revision to its effect. Inspect the output against the task instead of relying on the agent's success claim.
- D. Not quite. The skill workflow lets you revise and test instructions without training or replacing the model first.

</details>
