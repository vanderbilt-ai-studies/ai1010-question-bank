**Question:** You are deploying an agent to work on a project folder. Which setup follows the lecture's guidance?

- A. Give task-appropriate access and check important results
- B. Grant broad access after the model gives one reassuring answer
- C. Rely on a detailed skill to create any permissions it needs
- D. Choose the largest model and trust its report that actions succeeded

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. The tools and working area should fit the task. Inspecting important results checks whether the work actually satisfies the goal.
- B. Not quite. A reassuring response does not demonstrate reliable behavior across tasks or justify unnecessary authority.
- C. Not quite. Instructions describe a procedure. They do not create access that the surrounding environment has not granted.
- D. Not quite. Model size and a success statement do not replace checking tool outcomes and finished artifacts.

</details>
