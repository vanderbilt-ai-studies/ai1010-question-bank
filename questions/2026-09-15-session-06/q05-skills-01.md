**Question:** What does an agent skill provide for your work?

- A. Reusable guidance and supporting resources
- B. A trained model with replacement parameters
- C. A finished report containing the task results
- D. A conversation recording the agent's decisions

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. A skill packages instructions and resources so you can reuse and revise a procedure across tasks.
- B. Not quite. This confuses changing a procedure with changing the model. You can revise a skill without training a replacement model.
- C. Not quite. A report can be an output of using a skill. The skill supplies the reusable guidance that helps produce that output.
- D. Not quite. A conversation records an interaction. A skill is a reusable package that you can inspect and edit for later work.

</details>
