**Question:** What should you inspect alongside a skill's main procedure to understand the package?

- A. The model's learned parameter arrays
- B. The agent's latest completion message
- C. The editor's document appearance settings
- D. The procedure's supporting files

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Those arrays concern the model's training. The skill-inspection workflow focuses on the instructions and supporting files you can revise.
- B. Not quite. A completion message describes what the agent says it did. It does not replace reading the resources that belong to the skill.
- C. Not quite. Appearance can help you read a file, but understanding the skill requires looking at its procedure and supporting materials.
- D. Correct. A skill includes instructions and supporting resources. Read both, then compare the package with your intended task and criteria.

</details>
