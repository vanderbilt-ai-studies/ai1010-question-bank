**Question:** Which statement best explains why an agent's usefulness depends on the whole system?

- A. The model's language ability determines whether file actions are available
- B. The model, tools, and guidance work within the harness
- C. A detailed skill supplies both a procedure and the access it requires
- D. A connector supplies both service access and the complete task procedure

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. A capable model can propose useful actions, but the surrounding system must provide tools and access to carry them out.
- B. Correct. The lecture describes capability as a property of the parts working together. The surrounding environment affects what the model can accomplish.
- C. Not quite. A skill supplies guidance. Tools and permissions must also exist for its steps to execute.
- D. Not quite. A connector exposes an integration. The instructions for using it toward your goal are a separate part of the system.

</details>
