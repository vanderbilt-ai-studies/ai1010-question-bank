**Question:** What does alignment work aim to improve?

- A. The match between system responses and the behavior people intend
- B. The enforcement of limits on which external actions tools can execute
- C. The accuracy of a prediction about the next token in existing text
- D. The confirmation that a completed artifact contains the requested work

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. Alignment aims to make behavior fit intended goals and constraints reliably. Training can encourage that fit without guaranteeing it in every context.
- B. Not quite. This describes harness permissions and access controls. They constrain execution, whereas alignment addresses the behavior the model chooses.
- C. Not quite. This describes the foundational text-model prediction objective. Strong prediction alone does not establish that responses fit the behavior people intend.
- D. Not quite. This describes verification of a result. Alignment instead concerns improving the system behavior that produces responses and proposes actions.

</details>
