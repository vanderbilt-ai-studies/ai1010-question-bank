**Question:** You want a skill to give feedback that explains how to improve a result, but its instructions ask for a short verdict. Which revision best addresses the mismatch?

- A. Require a longer verdict and keep the current criteria
- B. Require a friendlier tone and keep the current criteria
- C. Require a restatement of the score before each verdict
- D. Require improvement guidance and test the feedback

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Extra length does not necessarily explain how to improve. State the specific kind of feedback you need, then inspect whether it appears.
- B. Not quite. Tone can make feedback easier to receive while leaving out improvement guidance. Revise the requested substance as well.
- C. Not quite. Repeating the score describes the evaluation but does not explain a path to improvement. Make that explanation an explicit requirement.
- D. Correct. Clarifying the desired feedback changes the procedure meaningfully. Testing lets you judge whether its explanation meets the intended criterion.
