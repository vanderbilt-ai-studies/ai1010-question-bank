**Question:** The class was shown the formal pseudocode for a decoder-only transformer. In it, what do the matrices written W_e and W_p produce?

- A. The probabilities over the vocabulary at the very end
- B. The weighted average that attention computes over nearby tokens
- C. The dense two-layer network inside each decoder stack
- D. The token embeddings, position included

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Those come from the softmax on the final line, not from these matrices. W_e and W_p are near the top of the pseudocode, acting before any decoder stack has run.
- B. Not quite. That is the attention step, which appears further down as its own routine. W_e and W_p run before attention and supply the vectors that attention then works on.
- C. Not quite. That network is the MLP (multi-layer perceptron), written with its own pair of matrices further down. W_e and W_p belong to the very first stage instead.
- D. Correct. W_e is the token embedding matrix and W_p the positional one, so together they map a sequence of token IDs into the embedding space with order encoded. On the pipeline diagram you already know, this is the token embeddings stage.
