**Question:** In Claude, the effort setting puts a limit on one specific thing. What does it limit?

- A. How varied and creative the wording of the final answer will be
- B. How many tokens it may spend thinking before replying
- C. How many words the visible reply may contain
- D. How much world knowledge the model can draw on

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. That is what temperature does, acting on the token probabilities at the very last step. Effort is about quantity of thinking, not about which token gets picked.
- B. Correct. Effort is a thinking budget: a ceiling on the tokens the model may generate for itself before it turns to your reply. The model may not use all of them, but it cannot go past them.
- C. Not quite. The budget covers the hidden thinking, not the answer you read. You can get a short reply after a long think, or a long reply after almost none.
- D. Not quite. World knowledge comes with the model you picked and does not move when you change a setting. Effort only changes how much work the model may do before responding.
