**Question:** In the transformer pseudocode, two weight matrices written W_mlp1 and W_mlp2, with a step called GELU (Gaussian error linear unit) between them, are the linear algebra for which component?

- A. The MLP (multi-layer perceptron), a dense two-layer network
- B. The multi-head attention mechanism inside each decoder stack
- C. The tokenizer that turns your text into token IDs
- D. The softmax that yields the probabilities over the vocabulary

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. Those two matrices with a step between them are the whole of the MLP in symbols -- the dense two-layer network sitting inside each decoder stack. The symbols are short; what they stand for are very large matrices of learned numbers.
- B. Not quite. Attention appears separately, as its own routine of about five lines. It moves meaning between tokens, while this pair of matrices works on a token's representation on its own.
- C. Not quite. Tokenization happens before the pseudocode begins -- its input is already a sequence of token IDs. These matrices act deep inside each decoder stack.
- D. Not quite. That is the return line at the very bottom of the pseudocode. W_mlp1 and W_mlp2 run much earlier, inside the repeated stack.

</details>
