**Question:** Why is it fair to say a reasoning model is generating text even during the stretch when your screen shows nothing?

- A. The tokens are produced by the same process; they are simply not displayed
- B. The model is loading its weights, which counts as preparation for generation
- C. The model is translating your prompt into an internal formal language
- D. The interface is buffering the answer and will release it all at once

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. Hidden and visible tokens come out of the identical loop over the decoder stack. Concealment is a decision about what to show you, not a difference in how the tokens were made.
- B. Not quite. Weights are already in place before your request arrives. What is happening in the pause is real token production, not setup.
- C. Not quite. There is no formal internal language being written. Thinking is ordinary generated text in the same vocabulary the model answers in.
- D. Not quite. Buffering would mean the answer already exists and is being held back. During thinking the answer has not been started -- the model is still producing material to answer from.

</details>
