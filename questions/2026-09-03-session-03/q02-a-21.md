**Question:** You have a fifty-item list to alphabetize and reformat as a table, and you want it back quickly. Following the lecture's advice, which tier fits and why?

- A. Opus 5, because reformatting work benefits from stronger reasoning.
- B. Fable 5.1, because it is the least likely to make a mistake.
- C. Sonnet 5, because the default should be used for anything structured.
- D. Haiku 4.5, because the task is mechanical and small is quick.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Complex tasks are where Opus earns its cost, and sorting a list is not one. You would wait longer and spend more compute for capability the job never calls on.
- B. Not quite. Fable is for the toughest problems and burns usage credits quickly. Spending it on formatting is the exact habit the responsible default is meant to break.
- C. Not quite. Sonnet is a safe answer, but the default is a starting point rather than a rule. When a job is this mechanical and speed matters, the tier below it fits better.
- D. Correct. The work needs no world knowledge and almost no reasoning, which is precisely the case a small tier handles as well as any other -- and faster.
