**Question:** A student assumes every quiz in this course will retest all the material from earlier weeks. What is the actual policy?

- A. Quizzes are cumulative by design, so each one covers the whole term so far.
- B. Each quiz is sealed off from earlier sessions and reuses nothing at all.
- C. Quizzes are not meant to be cumulative, though earlier ideas resurface.
- D. Only the final quiz of the term reaches back across everything covered.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. That would make each quiz longer and much less focused than intended. The aim is to check the new material while you have just met it.
- B. Not quite. This overcorrects. A question about model size cannot avoid the ideas that size is defined in terms of, so earlier material does reappear.
- C. Correct. The target is the new session, but the course is built so later ideas rest on earlier ones. When an old term shows up it is because the new material needs it, not because it is being retested.
- D. Not quite. No such special final quiz was described. The design point applies to every quiz: new material first, older ideas only where they are load-bearing.
