**Question:** A reasoning model works through a hard question and finishes thinking with plenty of its budget still unspent. What ends the hidden stretch and starts the visible reply?

- A. The thinking budget runs out and the mode closes
- B. The model generates a stop-thinking token
- C. The user sends a follow-up message
- D. The decoder stack finishes its last layer

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. A budget really does end thinking when the model reaches it, and then you get an answer built on unfinished reasoning. This model stopped with budget to spare, so the thing that ended the mode came from its own output rather than from a ceiling.
- B. Correct. The stop-thinking token is the partner of the think token that opened the mode. Once it appears, the model turns from writing for itself to writing for you.
- C. Not quite. Nothing you do interrupts the mode -- you are not even seeing it happen. The transition is generated by the model in the same token stream as everything else.
- D. Not quite. The decoder stack runs from top to bottom for every single token, thinking or not. Finishing it produces one more token, which tells you nothing about whether the mode has ended.
