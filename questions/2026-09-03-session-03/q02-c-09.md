**Question:** Why does raising temperature give you more varied or more creative output?

- A. It gives the model a larger vocabulary of words to choose between
- B. It makes tokens other than the top one likelier to be picked
- C. It lets the model deliberate for longer before committing
- D. It relaxes the grammar rules the model was trained on

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. The vocabulary is fixed and every token in it already carries a probability at each step. What changes is how likely the model is to leave the top of that list, not how long the list is.
- B. Correct. With low temperature the model tends to take the highest-probability token every time, which reads as safe and predictable. Turning it up flattens that preference, so surprising choices get through.
- C. Not quite. Deliberating for longer is the effort setting, a budget on thinking tokens. Temperature acts in an instant, at the moment one token is selected.
- D. Not quite. There are no separate grammar rules to relax -- whatever the model knows about language is baked into its probabilities. Temperature only changes how those probabilities are sampled.
