**Question:** In the transformer pseudocode, the line that reads MHAttention(...) stands for what, and how often does it occur in the model?

- A. The MLP (multi-layer perceptron), appearing once for the model as a whole
- B. Multi-head attention, one per decoder stack
- C. The softmax over the vocabulary, applied once at the end
- D. The positional embedding, applied once per token

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. The MLP is a separate piece written with its own weight matrices, and it is not what MHAttention names. Neither component appears just once -- both are repeated through the stack.
- B. Correct. MHAttention is multi-head attention, and it recurs once in each decoder stack rather than a single time. Notice how compact it is on the slide -- attention itself runs to about five lines.
- C. Not quite. The softmax is the final line, producing the probabilities. MHAttention sits inside the repeated block, well before any probabilities are formed.
- D. Not quite. Position is handled by the embedding matrix near the top of the pseudocode. MHAttention is the mechanism that moves a token's meaning using the tokens around it.
