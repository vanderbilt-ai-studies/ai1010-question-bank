**Question:** Claude's four model tiers, listed from the smallest to the largest, are:

- A. Sonnet 5, Haiku 4.5, Opus 5, Fable 5.1
- B. Haiku 4.5, Sonnet 5, Opus 5, Fable 5.1
- C. Haiku 4.5, Opus 5, Sonnet 5, Fable 5.1
- D. Fable 5.1, Opus 5, Sonnet 5, Haiku 4.5

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. Sonnet is the tier you land on by default, and a default feels like a starting point -- but being the default is not the same as being the smallest. Haiku is the tier built for speed, which is what puts it at the bottom.
- B. Correct. The ladder runs from the quick, small tier up to the enormous one. Haiku is sold on speed, Sonnet is the everyday default, Opus takes complex work, and Fable sits on top behind usage credits.
- C. Not quite. Opus and Sonnet are swapped here. Opus is the tier offered for complex tasks, so it sits above the everyday default rather than just above the fastest one.
- D. Not quite. This is the ladder upside down. Fable is the enormous tier that eats usage credits, so it belongs at the top, and the fast little tier belongs at the bottom.
