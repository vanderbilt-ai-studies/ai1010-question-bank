**Question:** What does the interface warn you about when you choose the Max effort setting?

- A. It is capped at exactly twice the thinking tokens of High
- B. It quietly moves you up to a larger model tier
- C. It halves the speed at which tokens are generated
- D. It can consume 3.5 times or more the usage of High

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Max is not described as a fixed multiple of anything -- it is presented as as long as the model needs. The warning attached to it is about usage, not about a token cap.
- B. Not quite. The model tier and the thinking budget are separate dials, and changing one does not change the other. A setting that silently swapped your model would defeat the point of having two controls.
- C. Not quite. Generation speed is a property of the model and the hardware, not of the budget. A long budget takes longer overall because more tokens are produced, not because each one slows down.
- D. Correct. Max means the model can think as long as it needs, and the interface flags that this costs 3.5x or more the usage of High. That is exactly why the take-home guidance was to stay at High or below.
