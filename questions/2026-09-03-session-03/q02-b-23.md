**Question:** The lecture said the thinking tokens are hidden from you rather than saying they are not produced. Why does that distinction matter?

- A. Because hidden tokens are stored for the vendor rather than the model
- B. Because hidden tokens are produced without consuming any of the model's capacity
- C. Because they are real tokens in the context: they cost time and shape the answer
- D. Because they are written in a different vocabulary from the visible ones

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. Who stores what is not the issue. The distinction is about the tokens being real generated text that the model itself uses.
- B. Not quite. They cost exactly what any token costs -- one full pass of the pipeline. That is why a long thinking stretch takes real time.
- C. Correct. Calling them hidden keeps two facts in view: the pause is generation happening, and the reply is built on what was generated. "Not produced" would lose both.
- D. Not quite. There is one vocabulary, and the model draws from it whether the token will be shown or not. Hidden refers to display, not to a different alphabet.
