**Question:** The lecture summed up thinking in one idea. Which statement captures it?

- A. The model is writing its own context before it writes to you
- B. The model is retrieving stored answers to similar questions it has seen
- C. The model is running its architecture at a higher precision for hard problems
- D. The model is checking its planned answer against its training data

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. Thinking gives the model more relevant material to condition on. Every thinking token it produces is in its context when the answer begins.
- B. Not quite. Nothing is being retrieved. A model does not keep a file of past questions to consult, and thinking produces new text rather than fetching old text.
- C. Not quite. The architecture does not change gear. The same decoder stack does the same arithmetic; only the destination of the tokens differs.
- D. Not quite. The training data is not there to be checked against -- what training left behind are the weights. Thinking adds new tokens to the context, which is a different thing entirely.
