**Question:** In the lecture the presenter acted out being asked a question, saying "hold on," listing several ways he might answer, rejecting one of them, and then replying. What was the point of the demonstration?

- A. That models are conscious in the same way people are
- B. That people should think out loud before prompting a model
- C. That the deliberating is itself just more text being produced
- D. That a model needs several attempts before it gets an answer right

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. The analogy is about the shape of the activity, not about inner experience. Nothing in the lecture claims a model has anything like awareness.
- B. Not quite. That is decent advice about prompting, but it was not what the demonstration illustrated. The subject of the demonstration was the model's behaviour, not yours.
- C. Correct. Talking through possibilities out loud and then answering is one continuous act of generating language. That is the claim about the model too -- thinking is generation, only hidden.
- D. Not quite. The demonstration was not about retrying a failed answer. It was about producing material for yourself first, so the single answer you then give is better.
