**Question:** Fable was not released as soon as it was ready. What reason did the lecture give?

- A. It proved unusually good at hacking.
- B. It generated tokens more slowly than the tier below it.
- C. It could not use the same tools the smaller tiers use.
- D. Its answers were less accurate than the tier below it.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. Along with solving hard problems and working for long stretches, that capability came with the size. It is why a model being ready did not by itself mean a model being released.
- B. Not quite. It certainly is not quick, but slowness is the expected cost of size and is true of every tier above the smallest, so it would not single Fable out. The hold-up was something the model could do, not something it was slow at.
- C. Not quite. Tooling is broadly shared across tiers, so a gap there is not the sort of thing that delays a release. The concern was what the model was unusually good at, not what it lacked.
- D. Not quite. Fable was described as having capabilities not seen in earlier models, so weak answers were not the problem. Some of those new capabilities were the problem.
