**Question:** A reasoning model spends a long hidden stretch on your question and then begins its visible reply. At the moment the first visible word is produced, what is in the model's context?

- A. Only your prompt, since the hidden stretch ended when thinking stopped
- B. Your prompt plus everything it wrote during the hidden stretch
- C. Only a condensed summary that the hidden stretch produced
- D. Your prompt plus a score for how promising each line of thought was

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. The stop-thinking token ends the mode, not the tokens. If those tokens vanished, the thinking would have bought the model nothing at all.
- B. Correct. That is exactly the payoff of thinking: the model has written itself extra material and is now answering with it in view.
- C. Not quite. No compression step runs at the end of thinking. The tokens are in the context in full, as written.
- D. Not quite. Nothing scores lines of thought. Whatever judgement the model expressed about them is itself text in the context, not a separate rating.
