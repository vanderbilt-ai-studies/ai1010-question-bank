**Question:** Which Claude tier does the picker flag as requiring usage credits, and offer for the toughest challenges?

- A. Haiku 4.5
- B. Sonnet 5
- C. Opus 5
- D. Fable 5.1

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Haiku is the smallest and quickest tier. Being cheap to run is the whole point of it, so it is not the one behind a credit gate.
- B. Not quite. Sonnet is the everyday default. A default cannot sit behind an extra payment, or most people would never reach a working model at all.
- C. Not quite. Opus is where cost starts to be something you have to manage, which is why the take-home guidance was to keep its effort setting at High or below. A credit gate is a stronger measure than that, and the picker puts it only where a single answer costs a great deal more compute than Opus does.
- D. Correct. Fable is enormous, and every answer it produces costs a great deal of compute. The credit requirement is the interface making that cost visible before you spend it.
