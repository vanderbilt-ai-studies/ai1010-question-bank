**Question:** You run the same drafting task on Sonnet and on Opus and cannot tell the two outputs apart. What is the sensible next move?

- A. Escalate to Fable, since the comparison came out inconclusive.
- B. Rerun both with a much longer prompt until a difference appears.
- C. Keep using the smaller tier for this kind of task from now on.
- D. Stay on the larger tier anyway, since more capability cannot hurt.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. Going up a tier when two tiers already agree spends credits answering a question you have answered. The larger models earn their place on harder problems than this.
- B. Not quite. Padding a prompt does not make a task harder in the way that separates tiers. You would spend more and reach the same finding.
- C. Correct. A task both tiers handle equally is a task that does not need the larger one, so the smaller is the right long-term choice here -- faster, cheaper, and just as good.
- D. Not quite. It feels like insurance, and that instinct is exactly what the responsible default is aimed at. You already have evidence the extra capability had nothing to do here, and you would pay its compute cost on every run from now on.
