**Question:** You move the effort setting from Medium to Max and then send a short, simple question. What does the higher setting guarantee about the model's thinking?

- A. Only that it may think longer, not that it will
- B. That the reply you read will be longer than it was at Medium
- C. That every token of the budget is used before it replies
- D. That the answer will be more accurate than it was at Medium

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. Effort sets a ceiling on thinking tokens rather than a quota to be filled, and on an easy question the model can stop far short of it. A budget is permission to spend, and the model spends roughly what the problem seems to need.
- B. Not quite. The budget governs the hidden thinking that happens before the reply starts, so a long think can still end in two sentences. Reply length and budget size are not tied to each other.
- C. Not quite. That would make the largest setting cost the same on every message, however trivial the message was. The ceiling limits what the model may spend; it does not instruct it to spend that much.
- D. Not quite. More room to think earns its keep on a hard, well-specified problem, but it cannot improve an answer that needed no working out. On a short question there is nothing for the extra tokens to do.
