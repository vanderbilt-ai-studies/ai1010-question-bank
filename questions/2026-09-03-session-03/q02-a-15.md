**Question:** Why does working with Fable burn through usage credits so much faster than working with a smaller tier?

- A. It charges a flat fee each time you open a conversation with it.
- B. It is enormous, and a model that size takes real compute.
- C. It always thinks at the maximum budget, whatever you set.
- D. It is billed by clock time rather than by the work it does.

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. Credit use tracks the work actually done, not a door charge. It is the sheer size of the model that makes each answer expensive.
- B. Correct. Every token has to be pushed through all of that model's numbers, so one answer costs far more computation than the same answer from a smaller tier. Credits track that cost.
- C. Not quite. The thinking budget is a separate dial from which model you pick, and it stays yours to set. Fable is expensive because of its size, not because it is locked to a setting.
- D. Not quite. Sitting idle in a conversation costs nothing. What costs is the computation spent generating, and a model that large spends a great deal of it.
