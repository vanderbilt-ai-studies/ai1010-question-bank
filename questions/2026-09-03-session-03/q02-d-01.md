**Question:** When you use a frontier model hosted by a company through its website, roughly how fast does it produce tokens?

- A. About 5 tokens per second, roughly a word every second
- B. About 60 tokens per second, sometimes closer to 100
- C. About 500 tokens per second
- D. About 3,000 tokens per second

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. That is the figure for running a model yourself on a laptop with no GPU (graphics processing unit). A hosted service runs on hardware built for the job, and that hardware is worth more than an order of magnitude.
- B. Correct. This is the everyday number to carry for a hosted frontier model. Hold it as an order of magnitude rather than an exact figure, so you can place it against the ~5 on a bare laptop and the thousands on a specialized inference chip.
- C. Not quite. That falls between the two figures the lecture gave. Hosted frontier models sit in the tens per second; the thousands belong to chips built specifically for this architecture, running smaller models.
- D. Not quite. That speed comes from a specialized inference chip running a medium-sized model, not from a hosted frontier model. Frontier models are large, and size is exactly what those chips cannot accommodate.
