**Question:** If you never open the effort menu in Claude, where is your conversation running?

- A. At Low, since a conversation starts as cheaply as it can
- B. At High, with a generous thinking budget
- C. At Medium, one step up from the lowest level
- D. At Max, thinking for as long as it needs

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: C**

- A. Not quite. Low is a setting you pick deliberately when you want an answer with very little deliberation behind it. The menu does not open at the bottom of the range; the five levels run Low, Medium, High, Extra, Max.
- B. Not quite. High was named as the ceiling for the take-home work on Opus, which tells you it is a level you move up to when the problem earns it. Opening there would spend the larger budget on every trivial message you send.
- C. Correct. Medium is the default -- where the menu sits until you move it, which is why most people never notice the setting at all. The levels run Low, Medium, High, Extra, Max, with a separate on/off toggle for thinking beside them.
- D. Not quite. Max does mean as long as the model needs, and the interface warns it can consume 3.5 times or more the usage of High. That is a cost you opt into, not one you inherit by leaving a menu alone.
