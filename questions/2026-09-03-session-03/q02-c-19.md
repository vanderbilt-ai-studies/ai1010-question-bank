**Question:** You want a fast, complete answer that leans on broad world knowledge and does not really need to be worked through step by step. Which combination fits best?

- A. A smaller model with a long thinking budget
- B. A larger model with thinking turned off
- C. A smaller model with thinking turned off
- D. A larger model at its maximum thinking budget

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: B**

- A. Not quite. That pairing suits a problem that mainly needs working through, where time can substitute for capability. Here the answer depends on what the model knows, and a smaller model knows less.
- B. Correct. World knowledge is what the larger model buys you, and skipping the thinking phase keeps the reply fast. There is nothing to work through, so there is nothing for a budget to buy.
- C. Not quite. This is certainly the fastest and cheapest option, but it is short on the thing the task actually depends on. Going smaller costs you world knowledge first of all.
- D. Not quite. You would get a good answer, at several times the usage and a long wait for deliberation the task does not need. The budget only earns its cost when there is real working out to do.
