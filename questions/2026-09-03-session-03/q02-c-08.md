**Question:** At which point in the pipeline does temperature do its work?

- A. On the token embeddings, before the decoder stacks
- B. On the context, as it is read in
- C. On the number of tokens the model may generate
- D. On the token probabilities, at the final step

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Embeddings are the model's representation of meaning going into the stacks, and temperature never touches them. Temperature comes in much later, once probabilities exist.
- B. Not quite. Reading the context is the prefill phase, and nothing there is being randomised. Temperature only has something to act on once the model has produced probabilities over the vocabulary.
- C. Not quite. That is the effort setting, a budget on quantity. Temperature does not lengthen or shorten anything -- it changes which token gets picked.
- D. Correct. At the end of the pipeline the model holds a probability over every token in the vocabulary, and temperature is noise added right there. Raise it and a lower-probability token becomes a likelier pick.

</details>
