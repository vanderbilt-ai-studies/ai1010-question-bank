**Question:** You set effort to High and then ask a one-line question. What does the model do with that budget?

- A. It may use very little of it, because the budget is a ceiling rather than an amount to spend
- B. It spends the whole budget, so the reply arrives once the tokens run out
- C. It shortens the visible reply to make room for the thinking it was allowed
- D. It raises the temperature to fill the time it was given

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: A**

- A. Correct. Effort caps how many tokens the model may spend thinking; it does not oblige it to spend them. An easy request can come back almost at once even at a generous setting, which is why the ceiling only really bites on the problems large enough to reach it.
- B. Not quite. That would turn every setting above Low into a guaranteed wait and make the dial useless for mixed work. The budget limits thinking; it is not a quota the model has to work through.
- C. Not quite. The budget covers the hidden thinking, not the answer you read, and the two are not drawn from one allowance. You can get a long reply after almost no thinking, or a short one after a great deal.
- D. Not quite. Temperature is a separate control acting on which token gets picked, and nothing links it to how much thinking is allowed. An unspent budget is simply unspent.
