**Question:** It is Saturday and you are working through the four-model comparison on Opus when you notice your usage draining fast. Following the guidance given in the lecture, what do you do?

- A. Move to Max, so the work finishes in fewer attempts
- B. Move to Extra, which is the setting recommended for take-home work
- C. Stop for the month, since the limit will not reset before then
- D. Stay at High or below, and wait for usage to come back

<details>
<summary>Show the answer and why each option is right or wrong</summary>

**Answer: D**

- A. Not quite. Max costs 3.5x or more the usage of High, so it drains the very thing you just noticed running out. Fewer attempts at many times the price is not a saving.
- B. Not quite. Extra was named alongside Max as a setting to stay away from for this assignment. Nothing in the guidance pointed you above High.
- C. Not quite. The wait is far shorter than that -- usage recovers within a day or so. Treating it as a monthly cap would cost you the weekend for no reason.
- D. Correct. High is the ceiling recommended for this work precisely so you do not stall, and if you do run out the usage returns later in the day or the next day. Waiting costs you time, not the assignment.
